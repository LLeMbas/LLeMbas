{% from "_macros.html" import icon %} {# The prototype panel: a page a model designed, open beside the conversation. Built like the panels beside it -- a child of .shell, `hidden` until something opens it, its own drag handle, the shared panel head -- and filled by nothing on the server. `prototypes.js` points the frame at the block somebody opened; there is no tab strip and no stored state, because every prototype already has a card in the transcript and that card is how you get back to it. The frame's address serves the model's page under a sealing policy (see services/prototypes.py). The `sandbox` attribute below is the second lock on the same door: either one alone keeps the page away from this application. Web and phone are two viewports for the same frame, not two renderings: the page is laid out at a desktop's width or a phone's, and the picture is scaled to fit the panel. A page with media queries answers to the viewport, so the phone view is the page's own phone layout and the desktop view its desktop one, however wide the panel happens to be. #}
